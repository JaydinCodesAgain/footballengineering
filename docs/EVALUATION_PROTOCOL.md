# Evaluation & Reproducibility Protocol

This document defines the engineering and statistical protocols enforced across the Football AI Engineering repository to guarantee reproducible, leakage-free, and non-fabricated evaluations.

---

## 1. Dataset Integrity & Fresh-Clone Reproducibility

Because extracted crop PNG files (`data/crops/<match_id>/images/`) and raw match videos (`*.mp4`) are excluded from Git via `.gitignore`, a fresh clone of the repository contains `manifest.json` and `manifest.csv` without the corresponding PNG files.

To prevent silent failures or mismatched labels:

1. **Integrity Validation (`JerseyCropDataset.validate_integrity`):**
   - Verifies that every `crop_id` in `manifest.json` has a readable image on disk whose pixel dimensions match `quality.width` and `quality.height`.
2. **Deterministic Coordinate Regeneration (`JerseyCropDataset.regenerate_missing_crops`):**
   - When crop PNGs are missing and the source match video is available, crops are regenerated by seeking directly to `frame_number` and slicing `torso_bbox = [left, top, right, bottom]` recorded in `manifest.json`.
   - **No live detector re-execution or random sampling (`rng.choice` / `rng.permutation`) is ever used during regeneration.**
3. **Dimension and Appearance-Consistency Verification:**
   - Each regenerated crop is checked against the manifest's recorded `width`, `height`, `grass_ratio` ($\pm 0.20$), and `blur_score`. (Note: because H.264/MP4 frame decoding across different OS/FFmpeg builds is not bit-exact, verification uses deterministic coordinate slicing plus dimension and appearance-consistency checks rather than cryptographic content hashing.)
   - If any crop fails verification (e.g., if a different video file was supplied), `label_verified` is immediately set to `False` and a `DatasetIntegrityError` is raised requiring manual reannotation. Old labels are **never** silently attached to unverified images.
   - If the source video is missing when images are absent, `DatasetIntegrityError` raises a descriptive error specifying the number of missing crops and the expected video path.

## 2. Classification Split Separation & Calibration Protocol

1. **Temporal and Identity Separation (`validate_no_split_leakage`):**
   - Datasets are partitioned chronologically into `train`, `val`, and `test` splits with an explicit frame gap (`min_frame_gap >= 5`) and tracking-ID isolation (`shared_tracks == []`).
   - For **Norway vs Wales**:
     - `train`: frames `16–752` ($0\%–40\%$ of video duration, $n=56$ positive samples + $13$ Unknowns).
     - `val`: frames `1632–1808` ($55\%–61\%$ of video duration, $n=30$ samples).
     - `test`: frames `1824–2080` ($62\%–70\%$ of video duration, $n=48$ held-out samples).
   - For **Chelsea vs Sunderland**:
     - `train`: frames `30–480` ($n=29$ positive samples + $4$ Unknowns).
     - `val`: frames `330–1410` ($n=47$ samples, temporally disjoint per class).
2. **Validation-Only Threshold Calibration:**
   - The nearest-neighbor cosine distance threshold (`unknown_threshold`) is calibrated exclusively on `val` via `TeamClassifier.calibrate_unknown_threshold`.
   - Held-out `test` samples are evaluated once using the frozen model and frozen validation threshold.
3. **Cross-Match Safety Guard:**
   - Classifiers operate on generic internal labels (`Team_A`, `Team_B`, `Referee`, `Unknown`) and store the `match_id` they were trained on.
   - Calling `predict_crop(..., match_id=other_match)` raises a `ValueError` unless `allow_cross_match=True` is explicitly passed for domain-shift experiments.

## 3. Object Detection Evaluation Protocol: Coverage vs. Recall

1. **Ground-Truth Provenance Enforcement:**
   - Bounding-box IoU metrics (`Precision`, `Recall`, `F1`, `AP@0.50`, `mAP@0.50:0.95`) require `FrameAnnotation` records with `verified=True` and `source="manual"`.
   - Importing annotations with `source` set to `"model_prediction"`, `"auto"`, or `"yolo"` raises a `ValueError`.
2. **Distinction Between Coverage and Recall:**
   - **Ball Detection Coverage:** The percentage of processed video frames in which at least one ball detection passes the confidence and spatial filters. Coverage can be measured on any unlabelled video segment, but **must never be reported as recall** (since a false positive on a white sock or pitch line increases coverage while reducing precision).
   - **Ball Detection Recall:** $\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FN}}$ computed via one-to-one IoU matching ($\text{IoU} \ge 0.50$) against manually verified ground-truth ball bounding boxes.

## 4. Ball Tracking & Possession Evaluation Protocol

1. **Observation Provenance (`observed` vs. `interpolated` vs. `lost`):**
   - The Kalman ball tracker (`src/possession/ball_tracker.py`) explicitly tags every frame with its provenance:
     - `OBSERVED`: Backed by a validated detector bounding box in the current frame.
     - `INTERPOLATED`: Coasting prediction during short occlusion/miss windows ($\le \text{max\_coast\_frames}$). Interpolated states decay confidence at each step and are **never** counted as genuine detector observations.
     - `LOST`: No detection within `max_coast_frames` or immediately following a camera cut.
2. **Possession State Machine & Exclusions:**
   - Valid states are `TEAM_A_CONTROL`, `TEAM_B_CONTROL`, `CONTESTED`, `LOOSE_BALL`, `UNKNOWN`, and `OUT_OF_PLAY`.
   - **Referees (`Referee`)** cannot own or control the ball under any proximity condition.
   - **Goalkeepers / Unclassified Players (`Unknown`)** are not attributed to `Team_A` or `Team_B` unless an explicit, verified goalkeeper-to-team rule is active; otherwise their ball contacts remain `UNKNOWN`.
3. **Two Mandatory Possession Metrics:**
   - **Attributed Possession Share:**
     $$\text{Share}_A = \frac{T_{\text{TEAM\_A\_CONTROL}}}{T_{\text{TEAM\_A\_CONTROL}} + T_{\text{TEAM\_B\_CONTROL}}}$$
     Computed strictly over intervals where one of the two teams has verified control.
   - **Total Analysed Time Distribution:**
     The exact proportion of total analysed duration ($T_{\text{total}}$) spent in each of the 6 states (`TEAM_A_CONTROL`, `TEAM_B_CONTROL`, `CONTESTED`, `LOOSE_BALL`, `UNKNOWN`, `OUT_OF_PLAY`), summing to $100\%$.
4. **Segment Scope Disclaimer:**
   - Every possession report records `analysed_duration_seconds`, `total_video_duration_seconds`, and `segment_coverage_pct`, explicitly stating that statistics apply only to the analysed broadcast clip/highlight segment and not a full 90-minute match.
